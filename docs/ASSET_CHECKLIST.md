# Replacing PhoneHub images

- The approved Lovable preview used a photo at `src/assets/phonehub-hero.jpg`. This source export substitutes `src/assets/phonehub-hero.svg`, and updates the hero import in `src/components/phonehub/storefront.tsx` accordingly.
- Replace the placeholder SVG by saving your licensed hero picture as `src/assets/phonehub-hero.jpg` and switching the import back from `.svg` to `.jpg` (or overwrite the SVG with your own appropriate SVG artwork).
- Phone illustrations on the cards are currently generated by `src/components/phonehub/phone-art.tsx`. Replace those with product photos after you have permission to use them.
- `public/favicon.svg` is a simple PhoneHub placeholder favicon.
- Replace all illustrative prices, stock statuses, customer promises, and contact information before launch.
- All payment, account, and admin results remain DEMONSTRATIONS. Do not accept real card numbers or use this frontend for live payments until a secure backend and provider are integrated.
